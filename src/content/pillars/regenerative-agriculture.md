The first arena of the work. The Doomer Optimism conversation about agriculture is rooted
in keystone-species restoration, the practical realities of farm economics, and the long
patience of soil-building. We platform the practitioners — farmers, ecologists, butchers,
seed-savers — who treat regeneration not as a marketing claim but as a daily craft.

Reading list anchors include Wendell Berry's *The Unsettling of America*, Wes Jackson on
the 50-year farm bill, and Chris Smaje's *A Small Farm Future*. Episodes in this pillar
are most useful when paired with the companion bibliography: titles, films, papers, and
field-tested resources we keep in the [research library](/library).
