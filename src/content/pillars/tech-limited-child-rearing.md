Hosted directly by Ashley Colby Fitzgerald, this pillar explores how families and
schools can resist the commodification and alienation of digital childhood — through
classical education, homeschool cooperatives, parish life, and the small daily
disciplines of phones-down households.

Our companion partners here include classical schools, homeschool coops, and parish
ministries. The work overlaps directly with Catholic Social Teaching's principle of
subsidiarity — the smallest competent community first.
