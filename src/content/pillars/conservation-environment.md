Reporting and reflection on the human dimensions of ecological upheaval: land use
conflicts, deglobalization, the politics of public lands, and the patient work of
restoring watersheds, prairies, and forests at human scale.

This pillar is anchored by James Pogue's reporting in *The New York Times*, *Vanity Fair*,
and *Harper's*, and by partnerships with land trusts and conservation practitioners doing
the unglamorous work of stewardship.
