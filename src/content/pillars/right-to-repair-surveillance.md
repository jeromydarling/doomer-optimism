The truck driver, the small farmer, and the home mechanic share an enemy: the
locked-down product that cannot be opened, repaired, or owned. This pillar is the
labor-and-liberty side of our technology critique, anchored by Gord Magill's
right-to-repair advocacy and the broader conversation about who owns the tools we
depend on.
