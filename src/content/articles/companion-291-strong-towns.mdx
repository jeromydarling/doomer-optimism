The case Chuck makes is straightforward: most American towns have built
infrastructure they cannot maintain on the tax base it produces. The
suburban-experiment pattern is a Ponzi only because the next round of growth
has, until now, paid the maintenance bill on the previous round.

The companion essay sketches the math, then turns to the more interesting
question: *what would it look like for one block — one parish — to begin
behaving like a strong town starting next Sunday?*
