## Summary

Matt P welcomes Matthew Perkowski, a longtime Twitter mutual and friend, to explore the intersection of technology, evolutionary biology, and embodied life. Perkowski brings a rare perspective shaped by work inside crypto and AI, alongside recent experiences stewarding six acres in Oregon and raising two young children. The conversation spans the genuine perils of technological abstraction—from crypto's psychological manipulation to AI's capacity to amplify our worst collective tendencies—while insisting these tools themselves are not the primary problem. Rather, the peril lies in our disembodiment: we have outsourced understanding to machines, collapsed complexity into team-based tribalism, and severed ourselves from the ground-level feedback that kept totalitarian tendencies historically constrained. Yet Perkowski articulates a clear path toward optimism: the careful, cosmo-localist work of building alternative communities that couple technological experimentation with deep embodied practice—land stewardship, family formation, and the slow accumulation of real-world wisdom that no tool can short-circuit.
