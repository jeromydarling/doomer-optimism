## Summary

Doomer Optimism's first live group chat brings together a rotating cast of previous hosts for an unstructured conversation about Paul Kingsnorth's "Against the Machine," AI development and its cultural co-option, and the urgent question of technology's role in childhood development. The discussion moves through a characteristic arc: the book provides intellectual scaffolding for naming what "the machine" is and how it absorbs all resistance to itself; the group grapples with whether accelerationism (letting everything play out) or guardianship makes sense given that we cannot actually stop technological development; and finally lands on a concrete, winnable battle—keeping smartphones and screens away from children until at least age 13, and ideally much longer. The episode captures both the fatalism and the practical optimism that defines the show: large-scale collapse may be inevitable, but parents and communities can still choose how they raise the next generation.
