## Summary

PlasmaRob and Ashley explore how Western civilization is built on Christian virtue, and how the postmodern rejection of universal truth has fractured our ability to reason together. They trace a historical arc from Enlightenment rationalism through postmodernism's takeover of institutions, examining how the loss of meta-narratives has led to derangement syndrome and the algorithmic amplification of outrage. But this is not a counsel of despair: the conversation reveals how natural law and virtue—understood as living well within real limits—offer a way back toward integrated human flourishing. The challenge ahead is recognizing what we worship, choosing life-giving sacrifice over illusions of control, and rebuilding trust in communities where children are raised as blessings, not economic units. There is no way forward except through something like faith—not naiveté, but the courage to live as if the old truths still hold.
