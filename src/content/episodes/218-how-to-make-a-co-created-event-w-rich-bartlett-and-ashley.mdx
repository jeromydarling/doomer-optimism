## Summary

Ashley Colby Fitzgerald has organized an upcoming two-day event in upstate New York bringing together authors Paul Kingsnorth and Matt Crawford to meet for the first time, along with roughly 50 attendees drawn from the Doomer Optimism listener base and adjacent networks—many of them skilled craftspeople, writers, and thoughtful practitioners working against machine logic. In this conversation with Rich Bartlett (event design specialist), Ashley and Rich work through the philosophy and pragmatics of co-created events: how to build genuine social fabric rather than passive listening; how to honor both the charisma of keynote speakers and the voices of quieter participants; how to calibrate structure just enough to enable spontaneity rather than control it; and how Ashley can step fully into her leadership role while remaining open to the group's emergence. The conversation moves from big-picture principles—clarity of intention, understanding norms in fragmented times, using food and shared meals as great levelers—down to tactical details: icebreaker formats, small group assignments, parallel session design, and the role of humor and informality in setting a convivial tone.
