A practitioner episode. Peter walks Ashley through the design logic of the farm —
how silvopasture, perennial polycultures, and large herbivore impact stack to
rebuild ecological function on land degraded by row-crop monoculture.

The episode pairs well with episode 278 (Chris Smaje) for the broader political
economy of small farm futures.
