## Summary

Mike Healy of [Wilmot Productions](https://www.wilmot-productions.com) joins Ashley to talk about small-scale American manufacturing — running a contract food production shop that takes other people's good ideas and actually makes them, at real volume, with a real crew. Wilmot does coating, repacking and packaging, dried fruit and candies, fruit-snack and gummy commercialization, and plant-based pet treats: the unglamorous middle of the supply chain where an idea either becomes a physical product or doesn't.

A conversation about what it takes to build something physical in a country that mostly stopped — and about the community that forms on a plant floor.
