## Summary

This conversation between Nate and Austin Frerich (author of *Barons*) charts the decimation of the American food system, particularly in the Midwest, through consolidation and regulatory capture. Frerich argues that the problem is not free markets but the absence of them—a rigged system where a handful of corporations (Walmart, JBS, Smithfield, Driscoll's, Fairlife) have captured regulators and eliminated competition. The conversation moves from diagnosis to possibility: rebuilding mid-scale farms, restoring animals to the land, enforcing antitrust law, and fundamentally reimagining agricultural policy. Throughout, both hosts insist that this work is not grim but energizing—a chance to fight for something better alongside neighbors and communities.
