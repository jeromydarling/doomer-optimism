## Summary

Should I stay or should I go? That's the question animating Andy and Keturah's early years of marriage—and haunting Patrick and Ashley's return home after seven years in Uruguay. The conversation tumbles through the territory that confronts anyone trying to live freely in America: runaway regulations, state surveillance, ideological capture of institutions, the atomization of extended family networks. But beneath the lament is something else: an investigation into agency, into what remains possible for those willing to ask hard questions and live according to their values. Whether it's the desert Southwest, rural Argentina, or fighting for local schools where you are, the hosts insist that settlements are tactical choices, not moral verdicts. The work is the same everywhere—building family life with intention, raising kids with roots that go deeper than screens, staying connected to kin. What shifts is the material circumstance, and material circumstance, as Patrick notes, makes you live a certain way.
