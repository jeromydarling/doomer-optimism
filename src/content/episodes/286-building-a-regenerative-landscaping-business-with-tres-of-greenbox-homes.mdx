## Summary

Tres of GreenBox Homes brings a decade-long journey from backyard garden experiments to launching a regenerative landscaping business that directly challenges industrial "mow and blow" practices. Born from a spiritual calling during COVID—"Make food"—GreenBox reimagines the landscaping industry as an opportunity to build soil, ecosystems, and community networks rather than maintain sterile suburban lawns. The business offers weekly maintenance hours to clients, enriches yards seasonally with native and edible plants, and is developing a membership network where residents begin sharing seeds, seedlings, and know-how. Rather than competing with conventional landscaping, GreenBox aims to replace it entirely, proving that environmental regeneration and financial viability can align.

What distinguishes GreenBox is its philosophical clarity: the goal is building living systems, not manicured aesthetics. This requires thinking differently about what "done" means—less input over time, not endless extraction. Tres articulates why this matters through a sharp critique of modern capitalism's abstraction from land and life, yet insists that business itself—properly constrained and community-rooted—remains humanity's most powerful tool for achieving things at scale.
