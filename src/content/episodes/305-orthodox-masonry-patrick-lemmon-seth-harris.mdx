## Summary

Patrick Lemmon and Seth Harris of [Orthodox Masonry](https://www.orthodoxmasonry.com/about) — a design/build firm specializing in structural masonry and timber frame construction — discuss why most American buildings are designed to fail within a generation, and what it takes to build alternatives. Orthodox Masonry's work creates buildings that are both structurally and aesthetically resilient, offering a deliberate alternative to disposable construction. The conversation explores the craft, economics, and civilizational stakes of building things that are meant to last.
