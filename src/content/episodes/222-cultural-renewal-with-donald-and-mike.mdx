## Summary

In this intimate conversation, hosts Mike and Don (Ashley Colby Fitzgerald) explore the intellectual, spiritual, and practical roots of cultural renewal. Don traces his unconventional path from punk rock and veganism through classical study to Orthodox Christianity, articulating a vision of collapse already happened—not future catastrophe but the present atrophy of literacy, public religious life, and children. Yet collapse is also opportunity. Renewal, he argues, happens one family at a time through beauty, books, and ritual. He describes his field school for high schoolers (no screens, Greek, literature, place-based learning) and his daily practice: simple but not easy—prayer, community, growing food, making beautiful things. The conversation circles through homeschooling, AI's ugliness, the difference between virtue ethics and utilitarianism, and why the ancient sources still matter most.
