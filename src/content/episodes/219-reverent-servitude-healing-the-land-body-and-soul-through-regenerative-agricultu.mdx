## Summary

Jake Hubbard, a 23-year-old regenerative farmer and co-founder of Brookhaven Farms, joins Ashley to discuss his philosophy of healing the land, animals, and community through agricultural stewardship. Raised with deep agricultural roots and mentored by iconic figures like Joel Salatin, Jake has built a multi-species operation producing grass-fed beef and lamb, pasture poultry, heritage breed heritage birds, and a 450-tree orchard—all managed with just two full-time workers. His vision extends beyond production: Brookhaven aims to become a model franchise-farm network, educational hub, and destination that proves regenerative agriculture can be both ecologically restorative and economically viable. Jake's doomer take centers on Big Agriculture's unsustainability and topsoil depletion; his optimism rests on nature's capacity to heal when stewarded properly, and on the transformative power of local food networks and consumer loyalty to small farmers.
