## Summary

Tom Murphy, a recovering astrophysicist and prolific blogger, joins Ashley to discuss his evolution from defending modernity as we know it to recognizing it as fundamentally incompatible with ecological health. The centerpiece of their conversation is Murphy's demographic modeling: he shows that UN projections have been systematically wrong about fertility rates because they assume populations will stabilize rather than continuing to decline. His models suggest global population may peak around 2040, with energy use—and thus economic activity—peaking even earlier, perhaps in this decade. Murphy argues this is actually good news, the most humane way to address human overshoot, but that institutional panic over declining growth will likely drive futile pro-natalist policies. The real work ahead is local, relational, and ecological: learning to live adequately outside the framework of growth, recognizing human supremacy as the root sickness of modernity, and cultivating something like a "religion of life" that honors the deep time wisdom encoded in evolution and ecological relationship.
