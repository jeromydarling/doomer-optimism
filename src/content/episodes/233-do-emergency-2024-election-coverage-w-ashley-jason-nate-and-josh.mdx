## Summary

In this DO Emergency episode, Ashley, Jason, Nate, and Josh convene to discuss 2024 election politics without pretending the outcome is life-or-death. All express ambivalence toward both major parties while defending individual voting decisions based on specific policy priorities—antitrust enforcement, regenerative agriculture, environmental regulation. The hosts resist the false binary of identity politics and make clear that Doomer Optimism itself remains nonpartisan, focused on prefigurative community work and bioregional projects that will outlast any electoral cycle. What emerges is a model of political engagement grounded in local action and philosophical coherence rather than team loyalty: figure out the answers now, build proof of concept, and be ready when the old systems finally can't hold.
