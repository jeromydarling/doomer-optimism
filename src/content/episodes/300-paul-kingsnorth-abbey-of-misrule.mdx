## In this conversation

Ashley and Paul revisit a question that has shaped both the show and Paul's writing
for nearly a decade: what *is* the Machine, and what kind of life can be made on its
edges. The conversation traces Paul's own movement from environmentalism through the
Dark Mountain Project to Orthodox Christianity, and turns toward the
practical disciplines of refusal — phones-down households, the parish, the garden,
and the patient work of planting trees you will not live to see.

It is a fitting 300th episode. The original premise of *Doomer Optimism* — that
unflinching realism about systemic fragility is compatible with grounded,
community-led hope — is what Paul has been arguing in long-form prose all along.
